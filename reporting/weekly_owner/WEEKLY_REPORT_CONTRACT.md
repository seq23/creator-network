# Weekly Owner Report Contract

Required sections:
1. Executive summary — planned/released/failed, pipeline, next-week plan, economic value, spend.
2. Employee scorecards — Marcus, Nia, Camille, Maya.
3. Last week — exact releases and outcomes.
4. Current pipeline — research/script/QA/render/ready/scheduled.
5. Next week — exact planned release count and major experiments.
6. What was learned.
7. Autonomous actions taken.
8. System health and self-healing — incidents, recoveries, unresolved issues, content coverage.
9. `OWNER ACTION REQUIRED: NONE` or precise human-only interventions.

The report may say HEALTHY only after applicable end-to-end health checks have been executed successfully.
