# Freshness Policy

- NONE: approved evergreen knowledge only; no external research.
- LIGHT: low-risk/current color; evidence pack max age 30 days.
- CURRENT: time-sensitive product/process/platform/current facts; max age 7 days.
- HIGH_STAKES: legal, medical, immigration, financial-outcome-adjacent or other regulated/high-consequence factual content; max age 24 hours unless an explicitly reviewed source-specific rule is stricter. Requires approved claims and independent QA.

Maya defaults to HIGH_STAKES for PI/legal, health, neuro and USCIS factual claims. Stale required evidence blocks PASS.
