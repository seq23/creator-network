# Assembly Contract

Output master: 1080x1920 vertical MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio when present. Captions/text overlays must be generated from approved content, not hallucinated during assembly. Assembly is deterministic: it may resize, pad, concatenate, normalize audio, and burn approved captions; it may not rewrite claims or scripts. Source assets and output receipts remain traceable to `creator_id` + `experiment_id` + `job_id`.
