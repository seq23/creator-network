# Security and Autonomy Boundaries

- Never commit credentials, API keys, OAuth tokens, recovery codes, or private inbox secrets. Store references only.
- Virtual employees may have public domain email identities; receiving routes may terminate at a human-controlled inbox outside this repository.
- Do not fabricate professional credentials, customer histories, wealth, degrees, diagnoses, legal authority, or guaranteed outcomes.
- High-stakes/current claims require future source freshness and QA gates before publication.
- Fail closed: a HUMAN_REVIEW or unresolved QA state quarantines an asset; it does not interrupt unrelated employees.
- Self-healing is deterministic and bounded. An LLM must not freely rewrite production code to recover from an incident.
- Budgets are hard caps. Exhausted paid capacity falls back to approved free/cheap formats or queues work.
- New virtual employees require owner approval initially.
